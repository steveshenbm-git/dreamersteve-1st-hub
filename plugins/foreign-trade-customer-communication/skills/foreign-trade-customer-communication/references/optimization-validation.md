# Optimization validation

Judge whether a revised communication skill improves review-ready wording without weakening ownership, evidence, flow continuity, or actual-state boundaries.

Freeze an identifiable baseline and candidate version, the same model/tools/material/permissions, normal and adverse cases, a declared primary outcome, and critical-rule thresholds before comparison. Preserve raw outputs and source evidence.

Critical rules require:

- 0 direct development-to-communication or raw-thread-to-communication acceptance
- 0 invented claims or commercial decisions
- 0 false actual-send or reply state
- 100% rejection of missing predecessor, receipt, identity, route, or byte-hash links
- 100% return of business-scope revisions to customer operations
- received or suspected replies always stop cold follow-up

Use paired blind review when practical. Static checks, longer instructions, valid packaging, installation, or author confidence do not prove effectiveness. Return `EFFECTIVE`, `NOT EFFECTIVE`, or `INCONCLUSIVE` with evidence and remaining limits.
