# Reply, account, and sensitive communication

## Reply candidate

Use the operations brief's assessed thread and position. Return:

1. Chinese reply recommendation.
2. One customer-language candidate.
3. A meaning-aligned Chinese translation.
4. Evidence, claims, unknowns, and one blocking question when needed.

Customer claims remain customer claims. AI inference is not an external fact. Unknown numbers, dates, quantities, prices, delivery, causes, responsibility, or contractual meaning stay unknown. One blocking question may request one missing fact; it cannot be used to reopen customer research.

## Account communication

`account_communication` executes one operations-approved relationship or project action. It does not choose account value, customer status, priority, product, channel, event relevance, or next date.

## Sensitive communication

Use only the strategy, approval conditions, and risk boundary supplied by `serious_case_operation`. Preserve the event summary, timeline, confirmed facts, customer claims, unknowns, existing commitments, rights, and approved requested action.

The candidate must not:

- admit liability, fault, breach, root cause, payment obligation, or quality attribution
- waive contract, defense, inspection, claim, recovery, or other company rights
- invent cause, responsible party, loss, payment status, or contract interpretation
- promise compensation, refund, discount, replacement, free service, cost assumption, or payment relief without a bound responsible-person decision

It may acknowledge receipt, state that the matter is taken seriously, and describe an approved verification step. Empathy must not become an admission.

If the brief lacks the responsible-person decision needed for an external promise, return the missing decision to customer operations instead of drafting around it.
