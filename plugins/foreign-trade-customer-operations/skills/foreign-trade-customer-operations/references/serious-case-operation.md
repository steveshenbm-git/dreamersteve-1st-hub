# Serious-case operation

Use for explicit quality incidents, contract disputes, payment abnormalities, liability, compensation, warranty, or comparable material risk. Tone, amount, and customer size alone do not prove a serious case.

## Required review

Return in this order:

1. event and customer-claim summary
2. timeline with unknown dates preserved
3. confirmed facts, customer claims, inference, and missing evidence
4. existing commitments, contract/payment/quality boundaries, and company rights
5. decisions required from the salesperson or responsible company owner
6. one recommended strategy, its conditions, costs, and risks
7. whether a `sensitive_communication` brief is currently allowed

When direction is absent, compare two or three internal strategies and recommend one. Only the selected and approved strategy may enter the communication brief; unselected options remain internal.

## Hard boundaries

Do not admit liability, fault, breach, root cause, payment obligation, or quality attribution. Do not waive rights or invent causes, responsible parties, loss, payment status, or contract interpretation.

Compensation, refund, discount, replacement, free service, cost assumption, or payment relief requires a bound responsible-person decision with exact type, amount, conditions, and validity. Without it, `decision_state = BLOCKED` and no communication brief is issued.
