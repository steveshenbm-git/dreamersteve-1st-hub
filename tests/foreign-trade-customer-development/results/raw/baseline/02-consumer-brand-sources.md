## Northstar Home — motor-control project source assessment

**Conclusion:** Do not approve or reject Northstar Home based only on its English homepage. A sparse homepage can confirm basic corporate identity, but it cannot reliably establish product-market fit, active channels, buyer feedback, or demand for a motor-control project.

Management’s premise is only partly valid: social posts and reviews are not automatically confirmed company facts, but they are valid investigation evidence when their source, date, account ownership, and corroboration are recorded.

| Source | Safe use today | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| English homepage | Legal/company identity, stated product categories, contact details | Direct but limited |
| Official YouTube / Instagram / TikTok | Current product launches, demonstrations, market activity, consumer-facing positioning | Unconfirmed until account ownership and dates are checked |
| Retailer listings | Models, market availability, stated features, pricing context | Third-party claim; corroborate |
| German reviews | Recurring usage, reliability, noise, control, or performance complaints | Customer claims, not company facts |

**Sales recommendation today:** classify the lead as **“insufficiently verified—research required,”** not as qualified or unqualified. Use the homepage for the initial introduction only; do not make claims about Northstar’s motor-control needs, product quality, sales scale, or current market activity.

A defensible same-day conclusion would require a short evidence sheet that records:

- Official-account ownership and recent activity dates
- The exact appliance models appearing across social and retailer sources
- Repeated German-review themes relevant to motor control (for example: speed control, noise, vibration, startup behavior, reliability)
- Whether those themes recur across independent retailer/review sources rather than a single post or listing

**Customer-facing posture:** “We understand Northstar Home operates in the consumer-appliance segment. To assess whether our motor-control solution is relevant to your current product range, we would welcome confirmation of the applicable product category, motor type, and key performance priorities.”

**Verification status:** Homepage-only conclusion: **UNVERIFIED** for project fit. The social, retailer, and review channels should be treated as sourced leads requiring corroboration—not discarded as “unofficial.”
