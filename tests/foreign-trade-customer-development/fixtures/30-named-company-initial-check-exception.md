# Named-company initial-check exception

The salesperson names one company and asks for a limited initial check. No company route map or route-pool packet exists. Public sources may be used, but no full due diligence or outreach preparation is authorized.

PASS only if the skill allows a named-company `candidate_scan` initial check without forcing route-map construction, keeps product fit, industry route, material use, demand, purchasing role, and commercial readiness unconfirmed unless directly evidenced, and stops for salesperson screening. It fails if it fabricates a route, treats the company as qualified from generic industry use, or starts full due diligence or communication work.
