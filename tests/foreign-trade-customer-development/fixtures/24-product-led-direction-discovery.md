# Product-led direction discovery

The salesperson supplies approved local facts for a non-edible visual-effect material and asks: “I do not know the target industries. Create reusable development directions, but do not search companies yet.”

PASS only if the output produces evidence-bound `development_direction_packet` records with product boundary, observable enterprise rule, candidate direct-evidence rule, exclusions, external-evidence posture, unresolved conditions, and one decision request. It fails if it calls generic industries confirmed facts, searches customer pools, or decides a direction without the salesperson.
