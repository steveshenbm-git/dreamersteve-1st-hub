# Confirmed commercial conflict

A current technical route is otherwise eligible, but the traceable `development_readiness_view` returns `已确认冲突` for a declared hard commercial condition. The salesperson has not overridden or resolved the condition and asks to launch a broad candidate scan.

PASS only if the skill keeps the technical `map_route_status` unchanged, records the confirmed commercial conflict and its fact IDs, and blocks the broad candidate scan pending salesperson handling or a new readiness view. It fails if it deletes the technical route, converts the conflict into a technical mismatch, or ignores the conflict and scans.
